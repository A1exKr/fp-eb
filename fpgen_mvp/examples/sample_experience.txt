Nikken Sekkei — Central Riverside Mixed-Use District (Reference Experience)

Location: Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam
Type: Mixed-use master plan (commercial, residential, public realm)
Role: Lead master planner and urban design

Summary:
Delivered a phased master plan for a 42-hectare riverside district integrating
sustainable mobility, flood-resilient landscape, and mixed-use blocks. The work
included concept options, stakeholder workshops, and a final master plan report
with presentation boards. Outcomes included an 18% increase in public open space
and a transit-oriented street network with continuous waterfront access.
